RECOMMENDED FIRST PRINT WORKFLOW
================================================================================

STEP 1: PRINT TEST PEG
  File:    (create temporary test peg from code)
  Time:    ~3-5 minutes
  Purpose: Verify pegboard fit BEFORE printing brackets
  Action:  Test peg in 2-3 different pegboard holes
           Should require firm hand pressure, not forced
  
  PASS:  Continue to Step 2
  FAIL:  Adjust peg size (sand gently) and retry

STEP 2: PRINT BRACKETS (In Recommended Order)
  Bracket 1: Power Strip (75 min) - largest/most complex
  Bracket 2: Govee (50 min) - medium, most important
  Bracket 3: Power Brick (45 min) - smallest/fastest
  
  Total: ~170 minutes (~2.8 hours)

STEP 3: TEST FIT & INSTALL BRACKETS
  □ Test fit each bracket on pegboard (use test pegs)
  □ Verify components sit securely
  □ Photograph setup
  □ Proceed to Step 4 OR Step 5 based on needs

STEP 4: PRINT CABLE CLIPS (Phase 2 - Modular)
  Option A (Minimal): Just Clip B (2x) for Govee + 1 spare
                      Time: ~40 min, Filament: ~6g
                      
  Option B (Standard): All 4 clips (1 each) for testing
                       Time: ~90 min, Filament: ~12g
                       
  Option C (Complete): 1A, 4B, 1C, 1D (typical demand)
                       Time: ~115 min, Filament: ~24g

STEP 5: TEST FIT & INSTALL CLIPS
  □ Measure actual cable diameters
  □ Place clips in pegboard holes
  □ Route cables through appropriate clips
  □ Verify secure routing and no kinks
  □ Final photography for documentation

TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK REFERENCE
================================================================================

PROBLEM                 | SOLUTION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Peg won't fit pegboard  | • Check pegboard hole size (should be ~19mm)
                        | • Sand peg gently with 150-grit (removes 0.1mm/pass)
                        | • Test fit in different hole
                        | • Verify pegboard not manufactured oversized
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Bracket shifts/wobbles  | • Check peg fit (should be snug)
                        | • Verify pegboard surface flat/rigid
                        | • Try adjacent pegboard hole
                        | • Reposition component to minimize load
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Cable slips from clip   | • Hole diameter too large for cable
                        | • Use smaller clip (B→A) or upgrade cable size
                        | • Sand clip opening tighter (careful!)
                        | • Double-check cable diameter measurement
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Clip won't stay mounted | • Peg loose; move to fresher pegboard hole
                        | • Peg worn; print replacement peg
                        | • Try pegboard hole that feels tighter
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Cable kinked in clip    | • Hole too small for cable diameter
                        | • Upgrade to larger clip size
                        | • Reposition clip to allow straighter cable path
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Print quality poor      | • Check nozzle cleanliness
                        | • Verify bed leveling
                        | • Ensure filament fresh/not moisture-exposed
                        | • Increase first layer print speed for better contact

For more details, see README.md TROUBLESHOOTING section

Before Troubleshooting:
  1. Read the appropriate README/CHANGELOG section
  2. Review print settings above
  3. Check component measurements (README.md specs table)
  4. Verify pegboard compatibility (19mm square holes)

Common Questions:
  Q: Will this work with my pegboard?
  A: Only if it has 19mm square holes with 3/4" (19.05mm) spacing.
     Round pegboards not supported. Measure actual hole spacing first!
  
  Q: Can I modify the design?
  A: Yes! Files can be edited in CAD software (FreeCAD, Fusion 360, etc.)
     for future iterations. Keep version numbers when modifying.
  
  Q: What if parts of my pegboard are warped?
  A: Brackets may not sit level. Use thin plastic shims under peg flanges
     to compensate for 1-2mm board irregularities.
  
  Q: How long will pegs last?
  A: Expected lifespan: 6-12 months with regular use.
     Replacement pegs are cheap to print (~3g each, ~$0.08).
